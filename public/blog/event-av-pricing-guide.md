# Event AV Pricing: What It Actually Costs and Why Quotes Vary So Much

![black flat screen tv turned on on white table - EventGear PDX](/blog/images/event-av-pricing-guide/cover.jpg)

> A practical breakdown of how event AV pricing works, what drives the cost up or down, and how to compare quotes that look nothing alike.

You've got a date set, a venue picked, and a rough guest count. Now you need a projector, a sound system, or a couple of wireless microphones, and you're asking vendors for quotes. One comes back at $300. Another comes back at $1,200 for what sounds like the same equipment. Neither quote explains itself in a way that makes the difference obvious, and you're left guessing whether one vendor is overcharging or the other is leaving something out.

## Why AV Quotes Look So Different for the Same Request

A quote built around just the hardware will always look cheaper than one that includes setup, testing, and a technician on site. Some rental companies price a speaker or projector as a standalone item and let you figure out cables, stands, and operation yourself. Others build the labor and support into the number from the start. When you're comparing two numbers, you're often comparing two different services, not two prices for the same thing.

## What Actually Drives the Cost Up or Down

Guest count matters less than room size and layout. A PA system for 40 people in a long, narrow hall costs more to get right than one for 80 people in a square room, because coverage and speaker placement are harder. Outdoor events usually cost more than indoor ones — wind and open air eat sound, so you need more power and often a generator. Multiple microphones, a hybrid streaming setup, or a screen large enough to read from the back row all add to the total. Rental duration plays in too: a few hours costs less than an all-day setup with a rehearsal window built in.

## The Real Risk of Choosing on Price Alone

The cheapest quote often doesn't include delivery, pickup, or anyone staying to run the equipment. If a speaker cuts out mid-ceremony or a projector bulb won't connect to a laptop, a self-pickup rental leaves you troubleshooting it yourself in front of your guests. That's not a hypothetical — it's the single most common reason events with AV go wrong. The cost of that failure isn't measured in dollars saved on the rental; it's measured in a ceremony nobody could hear or a presentation that never made it to the screen.

## What a Full-Service Quote Should Actually Cover

A complete AV quote should include delivery, setup, a test run before your event starts, a technician present for the duration, and teardown afterward. That's the model we use at EventGear PDX: every booking includes a technician on site from setup through strike, so you're never the one responsible for solving a technical problem during your own event. Rental length is flexible, from a few hours to multiple days, and pricing scales with what the room and the event actually need — not a flat per-head rate that ignores your venue.

## FAQ

**Why do two AV companies quote such different prices for the same event?**
Most of the difference comes from what's included. A bare equipment quote skips delivery, setup, and on-site support, while a full-service quote bundles all of it plus a technician for the event. Always ask exactly what's covered before comparing numbers.

**Does outdoor AV cost more than indoor?**
Usually, yes. Outdoor sound needs more power to overcome wind and open air, and setups often require weather protection or a generator, both of which add to the cost.

**Is it cheaper to rent just the equipment and set it up myself?**
It can look cheaper upfront, but you take on the risk of connection problems, feedback, or a failure with no one there to fix it. For anything with a live audience, that risk usually outweighs the savings.

**How far in advance should I get a quote?**
As early as you can, especially for weekend dates in spring and summer when inventory and technicians book up fastest. An early quote also gives you time to adjust if your room or guest count changes.

**Does rental length change the price much?**
Yes. A half-day event costs less than one with an early-morning setup and a late-night teardown, since labor time is part of what you're paying for, not just equipment time.

## The Bottom Line

AV pricing varies because the service behind the number varies. A quote that includes delivery, setup, testing, and a technician for your whole event costs more than a bare equipment rental, but it's solving a different problem: making sure your sound and video actually work when your guests are in the room. Ask any vendor exactly what's included before comparing price, and get your quote early if your event falls on a busy weekend.
